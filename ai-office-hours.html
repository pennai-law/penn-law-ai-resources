01 Where to Start

Start with what Penn provides, then add the model that fits your work. Which one fits is partly workflow, partly taste.

PennChat — new this year, and the safe default
Penn-provided · Free with your PennKey · High, Moderate & Low Risk
Claude and GPT models in one chat window, cleared for most University data. Connect on PennNet or GlobalProtect. No web search — bring your own sources.
Claude (Sonnet / Opus / Fable) — what I use ~90% of the time
Anthropic · Enterprise via ITS · Low & Moderate Risk
My daily driver. Best instruction-following and task completion of any model, and extremely extensible through Claude Code, skills, and MCP. Order Penn Enterprise Claude through ITS with research funds.
ChatGPT
OpenAI · Enterprise via ITS · Low & Moderate Risk
Widest adoption; strong all-rounder. Same footing as Claude: ChatGPT EDU through ITS with research funds.
Gemini
Google · Personal account · Low Risk only
Well-integrated with Google Search, and the best and easiest image generation of any model.
USE A PENN-REVIEWED TOOL CLEARED FOR THE DATA TIER.
Paying for a personal plan does not give it Penn’s institutional protections. Personal plans are fine for public or Low Risk work; for anything protected, use the Penn service cleared for that tier.
02 From Talking to Doing

The first wave was conversational — ask, receive, copy-paste. That era is ending. The more consequential shift: AI that does work, not just talks about it.

Then
Chatbot
  • You prompt, it responds
  • You copy output manually
  • One exchange at a time
  • Passive — you do the work
Now
Agent
  • Reads your files, writes new ones
  • Runs code, checks its own work
  • Works in the background while you don't
  • Chains steps toward a goal autonomously

Two tools that make this concrete — both available now:

Claude Code
Anthropic · Coding and file agent · In Enterprise Claude via ITS
Runs in your terminal, IDE, or the desktop app. Reads your files, writes and edits them, runs commands, and iterates — with your approval at each step. Built this page, and the AI Resources portal.
Claude Cowork
Anthropic · Desktop agent · Personal plan · Low Risk only
Hand Claude a task in the desktop app — research, draft, analysis — and walk away. It works in the background across the folders you connect. Delegation, not conversation. Not yet in Penn’s Enterprise Claude (under University review).

Think of these current AI tools as an extremely smart, very capable assistant who is also extremely literal. These tools are not search engines — they are collaborators who need very clear direction. The more you use them, the easier it gets to get them to do what you want.
